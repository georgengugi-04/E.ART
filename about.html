OUR STORY

The journey of bringing Kenyan artistry to the global stage

Our Mission

E-Art was founded with a clear mission: to showcase the rich artistic heritage of Kenya while providing a platform for local artists to reach a global audience.

We believe that art is a powerful medium for cultural exchange and understanding. By connecting talented Kenyan artists with art enthusiasts worldwide, we aim to preserve cultural traditions while fostering innovation in contemporary African art.

Our Journey

From a small gallery in Nairobi to a global digital platform

2024

The Beginning

Founded as a small physical gallery in Nairobi, featuring works from local artists with a focus on traditional Kenyan art forms.

2025

Expansion

Opened three more galleries across Kenya and partnered with international art exhibitions to showcase Kenyan talent abroad.

2025

Going Digital

Launched our online platform to overcome global challenges and create a borderless marketplace for Kenyan art.

2025

Today

Now a thriving global platform representing over 15 Kenyan artists with collectors from more than 7 counties countrywide.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Cultural Preservation

We are dedicated to preserving and promoting Kenya's rich artistic heritage, ensuring traditional art forms continue to thrive alongside contemporary expressions.

Artist Empowerment

We believe in fair compensation and recognition for artists, providing resources, education, and platforms that help Kenyan creators sustain their practice.

Global Connection

Art transcends borders and languages. We strive to connect Kenyan artists with global audiences, fostering cultural exchange and international appreciation.

Sustainable Art

We promote environmentally conscious art practices and support artists who use sustainable materials and methods in their creative process.
