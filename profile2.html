ABOUT THE ARTIST

John Njoroge is a passionate multi-disciplinary artist who brings together the ancient craft of pottery with contemporary painting and intricate mosaic work. With over 15 years of experience working with clay, John has recently expanded his artistic repertoire to include vibrant acrylic paintings and stunning mosaic installations.

Though new to the commercial art scene, John's work reflects a mature understanding of color, texture, and form developed through years of crafting functional and decorative ceramics. His transition into painting and mosaic art has opened new avenues for creative expression, blending his tactile expertise with bold visual storytelling.

John's unique perspective as both a craftsman and fine artist allows him to create pieces that are not only visually striking but also demonstrate exceptional technical skill and attention to detail.
